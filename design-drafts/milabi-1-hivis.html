Two ways to hire

Most requested

Wet hire

Machine, trained operator and fuel. You point to the work.

  • Operator works your site hours
  • Fuel and daily servicing included
  • Billed per day or per machine hour
For contractors with crews

Dry hire

The machine only. Your operator, your fuel, lower rate.

  • Handover check at delivery and return
  • Weekly and monthly terms
  • Breakdown support during hire

  1. Request

    Tell us the machine, the site location and how long you need it.

  2. Quote

    We confirm availability, transport and a written rate within a day.

  3. Mobilise

    Machine is delivered by low-bed and checked on site with you.

  4. Work

    Daily log sheets signed by your site engineer keep billing clear.
