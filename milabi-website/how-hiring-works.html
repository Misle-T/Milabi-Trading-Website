How hiring works

From your first call to the last log sheet: what happens, what is included and what you pay for.

Four steps

  1. Request

    Call, message or send the quote form with the machine, site location and how long you need it.

  2. Quote

    We confirm availability, transport and a written rate within one working day.

  3. Mobilise

    The machine arrives by low-bed and we inspect it on site together before work starts.

  4. Work

    Hours and fuel go on a daily log sheet your site engineer signs. Invoices come from those sheets.
